## C++ entry

```bash
# From Hostess7 / NewLatest package
./bin/field-ammoos online
./bin/field-ammoos status
./bin/field-hostess7 online   # full stack including AmmoOS
```

Binary: `field-ammoos` · ironclad `ironclad:field-ammoos-cpp:1`  
Serves `panel/field-desktop.html` as `/field` · full stack plates at `/api/status` · `/api/stack`.
